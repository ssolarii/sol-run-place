# World draft: refinement contract

This is Sol's miniature personal world, reached from the fast quicklinks homepage. Its places are Projects, About, and Quicklinks. Keep each reachable through real HTML links, keyboard input, and the simple view.

The chosen atmosphere is a slightly strange shaded archive: foliage, timber, pinned paper and red connecting thread. Sharp Helvetica Now lettering belongs to the existing site. Avoid sentimental garden copy, decorative particles, invented personal notes and generic project cards.

The scene carries the visual character. The interface should stay quiet: one persistent area navigation, small scene markers with labels on hover/focus, a clear way back, and readable paper content. Decorative numbering does not describe a required sequence and has been removed. Large titles use moderate tracking; regular text stays readable over the scene.

Foliage uses four actual fern clumps, prepared in Blender through the official MCP server and instanced with authored planting positions. Timber has softened edges. Material quality, the ground shape, and background depth remain active refinement work; a small polygon count alone does not prove good art direction.

Paper is intentional content, not a default card treatment. Demo notes identify themselves as demos. Missing biography, photographs and projects remain visibly unfinished until Sol supplies them. Peeling is reversible and affects only the visitor's local view. The private-message area does not accept submissions.

No automatic sound. Respect reduced motion, offer a manual camera switch, and keep simple view available if graphics fail or take too long. Music stays Lanyard first, Last.fm fallback. Page views stay Firebase/GA4 only; preserve internal campaign attribution.
