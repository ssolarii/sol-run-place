Shifty

Schedule Assistant is being rebranded as Shifty. It brings school schedules, calendar dates, and approved notices together for the Itz’at community.

What it does

The project centers on a seven-day school cycle, with lesson times, rooms, calendar dates, and school notices. It has separate presentation choices for students, teachers, and parents.
