# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People arriving from Sol's Instagram/TikTok bio links, and people exploring Sol's work and identity.

## Product Purpose

sol.run.place provides immediate social links and a personal portfolio with Projects, About and Quicklinks. Visitors can read about real work, inspect notes and see current/recent listening.

## Operating Context

The existing static site is published from ssolarii/sol-run-place through GitHub Pages. The fast homepage links to an optional richer route. Social in-app browsers and phones are important entry contexts.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Keep the homepage's quicklinks immediate.
- Preserve real project destinations, Lanyard-first listening with Last.fm fallback/history, Firebase/GA4-only page views and campaign attribution.
- Keep content readable through HTML, touch and keyboard controls, reduced motion and a simple graphics fallback.
- Notes can be edited through a content file. Visitor peeling is reversible and local.
- Implement real private messages and a separate moderated public guestbook with the existing Firebase project. Keep forms disabled until actual persistence, private/public access rules and integration are verified. Sol reads and moderates through existing Firebase console ownership.
- Do not invent biography, diagnoses, projects or personal notes. Missing content stays clearly unfinished.

## Brand Commitments

The existing name, self-hosted Helvetica Now lettering and dark tropical foliage are recognizable assets. The user wants foliage without sentimental or cozy treatment. The replacement uses multiple textured shallow 3D leaves with restrained mouse and scroll response, carved wooden frames and physically bending notes in a composed depth hierarchy. Movement and section transitions use small X/Y changes and little or no Z camera travel. Phones retain normal scrolling. Remove visible Simple view, Illustrative sample and WIP/Work in progress labels; provenance belongs in development files.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing foliage photograph, fonts and social icons; an authentic Schedule Assistant screenshot and verified Shifty project context; real Last.fm history. Fuller biography, personal photographs and additional project stories are not supplied.

## Product Principles

- Immediate access before exploration.
- Personal content earns its place.
- Physical depth supports clarity.
- Honest pending states instead of invented content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable HTML navigation, visible focus, touch-sized controls, no surprise audio, reduced motion and a usable simple view.
